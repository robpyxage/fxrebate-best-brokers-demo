Choose a prop firm by checking its contract, loss limits and payout rules before comparing account sizes or discounts. This guide covers online retail evaluation and funded-trader programmes, which can differ significantly from traditional proprietary trading jobs.

01

Understand what you are buying

Establish whether each stage uses simulated funds or a live trading account. Passing a challenge does not necessarily lead to live trading: some programmes pay rewards based on simulated performance. A headline account size is not money you own or can withdraw. Check the evaluation stages, progression conditions and the agreement that applies after passing.

02

Verify the company and its terms

Identify the legal entity, location, country restrictions and dispute process. Company registration is not financial-services authorisation, and a partner broker’s licence does not automatically cover the prop firm. Verify any regulatory claims with the relevant authority and check official warnings. Do not assume challenge fees or unpaid rewards have investor compensation protection.

03

Make drawdown rules your first comparison

Drawdown rules define how much the account can lose before a breach. Ask whether limits use balance or equity, include open losses and fees, and change after profits or payouts. A daily limit may reset in a different time zone from yours. Read the rules for both the evaluation and reward-eligible stages.

Rule typeWhat it means for your account
Static maximum lossThe loss threshold stays tied to a fixed reference, usually the initial balance.
Trailing maximum lossThe threshold moves up with a defined peak balance or equity; check when it stops trailing.
Daily loss limitA separate daily restriction; check its reference, reset time and treatment of open positions.

A simple drawdown example: Assume a $100,000 account and a $6,000 loss allowance. A static floor is $94,000. Under an uncapped trailing rule based on the highest end-of-day balance, a close at $104,000 raises the floor to $98,000. These are hypothetical rules; confirm whether touching the threshold counts as a breach.

End-of-day trailing describes when a threshold is updated. It does not necessarily mean breaches are checked only at the end of the day; open positions may still trigger an intraday breach.

04

Compare costs and payout conditions

Estimate the cost of a realistic attempt, not just the discounted entry fee. Check whether subscriptions renew automatically and when they stop. Treat any fee refund as conditional until its requirements are met.

Include every condition: Record reset, activation, platform and data fees. For payouts, check minimum days, consistency rules, the trader profit share, payout caps, required buffers and processing charges. Confirm whether a payout changes your loss threshold.

Neither guarantees a pass or reward. If you fail, any rebate entitlement still depends on the specific offer terms.

06

Check that your strategy is permitted

Review news trading, overnight and weekend holding, automated strategies, copying, position limits and prohibited practices. Check profit targets, minimum trading days, time limits and inactivity rules. A consistency rule may limit how much of your profit can come from one day or trade. Rules can differ between evaluation and funded stages.

Confirm the instruments, platform, trading hours, spreads and commissions you will use. Ask support to explain ambiguous restrictions in writing before paying, including when a rule breach can cancel an account or a payout.

07

Build your prop firm shortlist

Compare the exact programme and account size, not only the firm’s brand. Save the dated rules and agreement before purchase, and record any differences between the evaluation and reward-eligible account.

08

Before paying for a challenge

Assess reliability carefully: Look for recurring, specific complaints about rejected payouts or changing rules, and test support with a detailed question. Payout screenshots, affiliate rankings and testimonials alone do not prove reliability. Understand the firm’s right to change terms and your options if it stops operating.

09

Access FXRebate challenge benefits

Look for a Challenge Discount to lower the purchase price or a Challenge Acquisition Rebate to recover part of an eligible purchase. Check availability for your chosen firm and programme, then follow the offer’s referral link or code and any claim instructions. Verify the calculation basis, eligibility, payment timing and whether offers can be combined. These benefits are separate from a prop firm’s own fee refund and do not change its trading or payout rules.
