A SHARED AMBITION

Meaningful work, shared ambition.

FXRebate is a global cashback, rebate, comparison, and education platform serving traders, affiliates, and financial-industry partners worldwide.

Our team brings together professionals with more than 20 years of experience in financial markets, trading, brokerage partnerships, financial technology, operations, and client services. We combine this industry knowledge with a practical, technology-driven approach to help eligible clients reduce their trading costs and better understand the brokers, crypto exchanges, prop firms, and other services they use.

As FXRebate continues to expand, we are looking for talented, responsible, and motivated people who want to help us improve the way traders access cashback, provider information, financial education, and partner services.

01 / LIFE AT FXREBATE

Why Work at FXRebate?

FXRebate operates at the intersection of financial markets, technology, digital marketing, client services, and international partnerships.

Working with us means contributing to a platform that connects:

  • Forex and CFD traders
  • crypto traders and exchanges
  • proprietary trading firms
  • Introducing Brokers and Affiliates
  • financial-service providers
  • educational and technology partners
  • clients from multiple countries and jurisdictions.

Our work is international, fast-moving, and continuously evolving. Team members have the opportunity to gain practical experience across financial markets, fintech products, customer experience, partnerships, compliance, digital platforms, and online business development.

02 / LIFE AT FXREBATE

Our Working Culture

01

Client-Focused

We aim to make FXRebate services clear, reliable, and easy to use. Every team member contributes to the quality of the client and partner experience.

02

Transparent

We value honest communication, accurate information, and clearly defined responsibilities. We expect our team members to communicate openly and raise concerns when something requires attention.

03

Responsible

FXRebate operates in an industry where accuracy, confidentiality, security, and compliance are essential. We look for people who take ownership of their work and understand the importance of handling client and partner information responsibly.

04

Collaborative

Our departments work closely together. Client support, partnerships, payments, product development, marketing, research, compliance, and technology must remain aligned to provide a consistent service.

05

International

FXRebate works with clients and companies across different countries, cultures, languages, and regulatory environments. We value cultural awareness, adaptability, and strong communication skills.

06

Continuously Improving

Financial markets, technology, partner requirements, and regulations change constantly. We encourage team members to learn, improve processes, identify problems, and propose better solutions.

03 / LIFE AT FXREBATE

Areas Where You Can Build Your Career

FXRebate may recruit for roles across the following areas:

01 / CAREER AREAClient Support and Account Assistance+

Help clients understand how FXRebate works, link eligible accounts, resolve account-related questions, and access available rebates and partner services.

Typical responsibilities may include:

  • responding to client enquiries
  • assisting with account registration and linking procedures
  • explaining rebate conditions and payment methods
  • coordinating with broker and partner support teams
  • escalating technical, payment, or compliance matters
  • maintaining clear and professional communication.
02 / CAREER AREAPartnerships and Business Development+

Develop and maintain relationships with Forex brokers, crypto exchanges, prop firms, technology providers, and other financial-industry partners.

Typical responsibilities may include:

  • identifying potential partners
  • negotiating IB, Affiliate, CPA, PPL, SCR, and Revenue Share arrangements
  • maintaining partner CRM information
  • coordinating onboarding and account-management activities
  • reviewing commission structures and jurisdictional restrictions
  • supporting joint promotions, contests, and marketing campaigns.
03 / CAREER AREARebate Calculation, Payments, and Reconciliation+

Support the accurate calculation, verification, and payment of client rebates and Affiliate commissions.

Typical responsibilities may include:

  • reviewing broker and partner reports
  • reconciling commissions and trading activity
  • validating rebate calculations
  • processing payment requests
  • managing payment-method verification
  • investigating discrepancies, reversals, or failed payments
  • maintaining accurate financial and operational records.
04 / CAREER AREATechnology and Product Development+

Help build and improve the FXRebate website, Client Area, Affiliate Portal, Partner CRM, calculators, comparison tools, payment processes, and security systems.

Typical opportunities may include:

  • frontend and backend development
  • database and API integration
  • UI and UX design
  • quality assurance and testing
  • cybersecurity and account protection
  • data processing and reporting
  • CRM development
  • automation and internal tools.
05 / CAREER AREAResearch, Ratings, and Content+

Research brokers, crypto exchanges, prop firms, regulations, trading conditions, fees, platforms, promotions, and industry developments.

Typical responsibilities may include:

  • maintaining provider profiles
  • verifying regulatory and company information
  • writing educational guides and reviews
  • updating ratings and comparison data
  • researching promotions and rebate structures
  • creating market and industry content
  • supporting the FXRebate Trading Academy and Financial Dictionary.
06 / CAREER AREAMarketing and Communications+

Develop campaigns that introduce FXRebate services to traders, Affiliates, and financial-industry partners.

Typical responsibilities may include:

  • email marketing
  • social media
  • SEO and AEO content
  • paid advertising
  • campaign management
  • brand communications
  • partner promotions
  • newsletters
  • community development
  • performance analysis.
07 / CAREER AREALegal, Compliance, and Privacy+

Support FXRebate in maintaining responsible business practices across multiple jurisdictions and partner relationships.

Typical responsibilities may include:

  • reviewing contracts and partnership agreements
  • supporting KYC and KYB procedures
  • monitoring country and service restrictions
  • maintaining legal policies and disclosures
  • handling privacy and data-protection matters
  • investigating complaints or suspected abuse
  • coordinating with advisers, partners, and authorities.
08 / CAREER AREAAffiliate and SUB-IB Management+

Support FXRebate Affiliates, referral partners, and SUB-IB relationships.

Typical responsibilities may include:

  • Affiliate onboarding
  • commission and tier administration
  • referral-tracking support
  • marketing-compliance reviews
  • Affiliate communication
  • performance reporting
  • broker-specific SUB-IB opportunities.
Who We Are Looking For+

The ideal FXRebate team member is:

  • professional and reliable
  • comfortable working in an international environment
  • interested in financial markets, fintech, or digital business
  • attentive to detail
  • able to communicate clearly
  • willing to take responsibility
  • respectful of confidentiality and data security
  • capable of working independently and as part of a team
  • motivated to learn and improve
  • focused on practical solutions.

Experience in Forex, CFDs, crypto, proprietary trading, brokerage, fintech, payments, client support, digital marketing, software development, or financial compliance is valuable, but not required for every position.

For junior roles, we also value curiosity, discipline, language skills, analytical thinking, and a genuine interest in learning about the industry.

Languages and International Experience+

Because FXRebate operates internationally, additional languages are an advantage.

Depending on the role, we may value candidates who can communicate professionally in:

  • English
  • Romanian
  • Spanish
  • French
  • Arabic
  • Chinese
  • German
  • Italian
  • Portuguese
  • other languages relevant to our clients and partners.

Knowledge of international markets, cultural differences, and regional financial-service practices can also be beneficial.

What We Offer+

The exact employment or collaboration conditions depend on the position, experience level, location, and contractual arrangement.

Depending on the role, FXRebate may offer:

  • work in an international financial-services environment
  • exposure to Forex, crypto, prop trading, fintech, and Affiliate businesses
  • opportunities to work with global partners
  • professional development and industry learning
  • involvement in new products and platform development
  • opportunities to contribute ideas and improve processes
  • flexible or remote working arrangements where appropriate
  • performance-based development opportunities
  • a collaborative and entrepreneurial working environment.

Specific compensation, working hours, benefits, and location requirements will be communicated during the recruitment process.

09 / LIFE AT FXREBATE

Recruitment Process

The recruitment process may include:

01

1. Application Review

We review the candidate’s experience, skills, language capabilities, and suitability for the relevant role.

02

2. Initial Discussion

Selected candidates may be invited to an introductory interview to discuss their experience, expectations, availability, and understanding of FXRebate’s business.

03

3. Skills or Practical Assessment

Depending on the position, candidates may be asked to complete a reasonable practical task, technical test, writing exercise, case study, or role-specific assessment.

04

4. Final Interview

The final discussion may involve the relevant department manager and focus on responsibilities, working conditions, team fit, compensation, and the next steps.

05

5. Offer and Onboarding

Successful candidates receive a written offer or collaboration proposal describing the applicable terms.

Equal Opportunities+

FXRebate values professional ability, integrity, responsibility, and performance.

We aim to assess candidates fairly and do not tolerate unlawful discrimination based on characteristics protected by applicable law.

Recruitment decisions are based on the requirements of the role, the candidate’s experience and skills, and the needs of the business.

Privacy and Recruitment Data+

Personal information submitted during recruitment will be used only for legitimate recruitment, evaluation, communication, legal, and recordkeeping purposes.

Candidate information may include:

  • contact details
  • CV and employment history
  • education and qualifications
  • interview notes
  • assessment results
  • professional references
  • salary expectations
  • right-to-work or identification information where required.

Recruitment data will be processed in accordance with the FXRebate Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection legislation.

Candidates should not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information in their application.

Recruitment Security Notice+

FXRebate will never ask a candidate to:

  • pay a recruitment fee
  • purchase equipment or software from a specified person
  • transfer cryptocurrency as part of recruitment
  • provide broker-account passwords
  • provide email passwords or security codes
  • open a trading account as a condition of employment
  • send funds to secure an interview or position.

Official recruitment communications will be sent only through contact details published on the FXRebate website or through verified representatives of Financial Trading Art.

If you receive a suspicious recruitment message claiming to represent FXRebate, please contact us through the official website before providing any information.
