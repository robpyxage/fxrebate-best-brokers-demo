OUR PURPOSE

More value for the journey.

FXRebate is an independent cashback, rebate, referral, comparison, and education platform serving traders, affiliates, companies, and financial-industry partners worldwide.

Our purpose is straightforward: when an eligible client uses a partner broker, crypto exchange, prop firm, or other financial-service provider through an approved FXRebate arrangement, the partner may pay FXRebate a commission. Depending on the applicable program, we return part of that commission to the client as cashback, a trading rebate, a spread or commission reduction, or a direct discount.

FXRebate does not manage client funds, execute trades, provide portfolio management, or make trading decisions on behalf of clients. Trading accounts, deposits, withdrawals, execution, and financial products remain under the control and responsibility of the relevant third-party provider.

01 / THE FXREBATE STORY

Our Story

FXRebate has been active since 2011 and is headquartered in Bucharest, Romania. The business was built around a simple idea: traders already generate commercial value for brokers and other providers through their eligible activity, so part of that value can be returned to them instead of remaining entirely within the traditional distribution chain.

Over time, FXRebate has developed from a Forex-focused cashback service into a broader platform covering:

  • Forex and CFD brokers
  • crypto exchanges
  • proprietary trading firms
  • affiliate and SUB-IB programs
  • partner promotions and contests
  • ratings, reviews, and comparison resources
  • trading education and market insights
  • trading tools, software, and related services.

FXRebate’s public company profile identifies the business as privately held, based in Bucharest, and founded in 2011. Financial Trading Art SRL was incorporated in Romania in February 2011.

Explore how the model works +

Our model is based on commercial partnerships with brokers, exchanges, prop firms, and other service providers.

A typical rebate arrangement works as follows:

A client registers with FXRebate and selects an eligible partner.

Depending on the partner’s rules, the client opens a new account or links an existing account under the FXRebate partner structure.

The client continues using the partner’s services normally.

The partner reports qualifying activity and pays FXRebate the applicable IB, affiliate, volume-based, CPA, revenue-share, or other agreed commission.

FXRebate verifies the information and returns the applicable portion to the client.

Rebate structures differ between partners. They may be based on trading volume, spread or commission, CPA qualification, partner revenue, challenge purchases, combined arrangements, or other partner-specific conditions. Some benefits are paid as cash, while others may be delivered as reduced trading costs, direct account credits, challenge discounts, software access, or educational resources.

03 / THE FXREBATE STORY

What We Offer

01

Forex Broker Rebates

Eligible traders can receive cashback based on qualifying activity with participating Forex and CFD brokers. The rebate may be calculated per lot, as a percentage of the spread or commission, through revenue share, or through another broker-specific model.

02

Crypto Exchange Rebates

Where supported by the relevant exchange partnership, eligible clients may receive cashback linked to qualifying spot, margin, futures, or other trading activity. Eligibility, maker and taker fee treatment, supported products, and payout conditions vary by exchange.

03

Prop Firm Rebates and Discounts

For selected proprietary trading firms, FXRebate may provide cashback after an eligible Challenge or program purchase, or offer the benefit directly as a reduced acquisition price. The prop firm’s evaluation rules, drawdown limits, funding conditions, profit split, and payout terms remain unchanged.

04

Ratings, Reviews, and Comparisons

FXRebate provides structured information designed to help users research and compare Forex brokers, crypto exchanges, and prop firms. Our platform brings together information such as regulation, fees, platforms, account types, trading conditions, features, promotions, restrictions, rebates, and client feedback.

05

Promotions and Contests

Partners may publish eligible bonuses, promotions, contests, loyalty programs, cashback campaigns, and prop firm discounts through FXRebate. Availability may depend on country, legal entity, account type, client classification, and partner rules.

06

Trading Tools and Education

FXRebate provides educational guides, broker and platform information, market insights, trading strategies, rebate calculators, market tools, and other resources intended to help users understand trading costs, provider structures, and financial-market concepts. The FXRebate Blog currently covers broker reviews, rebate education, trading platforms, market developments, crypto exchange costs, and prop firm rules.

Our Affiliate Community+

FXRebate also operates a multi-tier Affiliate Program.

Eligible affiliates can refer clients through their unique tracking links and earn commissions based on qualifying rebates generated by those referrals. The standard structure may include up to three levels:

  • Tier 1 — directly referred users
  • Tier 2 — users referred by Tier 1 affiliates
  • Tier 3 — users referred by Tier 2 affiliates.

Affiliate earnings are linked to genuine qualifying activity and confirmed rebates, not simply to recruitment or account registrations. Actual commission percentages and eligibility conditions are displayed in the Affiliate Portal or agreed separately in writing.

08 / THE FXREBATE STORY

What We Stand For

01

Transparency

We aim to explain how rebates are calculated, when they are paid, what conditions apply, and which legal entity or partner arrangement is relevant.

02

Client Control

Clients retain control over their trading accounts, funds, strategies, and provider relationships. FXRebate does not require authority to place trades or manage client capital.

03

Partner-Specific Accuracy

Rebate rates and eligibility can vary by partner, legal entity, country, account type, instrument, and qualifying activity. We aim to present these conditions clearly rather than treating every provider arrangement as identical.

04

Responsible Communication

Cashback can reduce eligible costs, but it does not remove trading risk, guarantee profitability, or compensate for poor risk management. Clients should never increase their trading volume solely to earn rebates.

05

Long-Term Relationships

We aim to build sustainable relationships with clients and partners through clear communication, reliable reconciliation, practical support, and commercially fair arrangements.

06

Continuous Improvement

Financial markets, technology, regulation, and partner structures evolve continuously. FXRebate continues to develop its platform, security controls, educational resources, ratings, tools, and partner-management systems to meet these changing requirements.

More Than a Rebate Platform+

FXRebate is designed to bring several parts of the trader journey into one ecosystem:

  • discovering and comparing providers
  • understanding regulation, costs, platforms, and account conditions
  • activating eligible rebates or discounts
  • following promotions and industry updates
  • accessing educational content and trading tools
  • earning additional commission through the Affiliate Program
  • receiving support when linking or administering eligible accounts.

Our goal is not to encourage clients to trade more. It is to help eligible clients understand their options, reduce qualifying costs where possible, and make better-informed decisions about the providers and services they use.

Important Service Clarification+

FXRebate is not a broker, crypto exchange, prop firm, bank, payment institution, portfolio manager, or investment adviser.

We do not:

  • accept trading deposits
  • hold client margin
  • execute or transmit trading orders
  • manage trading accounts
  • determine market prices
  • guarantee partner performance
  • guarantee rebates or financial outcomes
  • provide personalized investment, legal, or tax advice.

All trading, exchange, funding, Challenge, and investment services are provided directly by independent third-party partners under their own agreements, rules, licences, and risk disclosures.
