01

What Are Prop Firm Rebates?

Prop firm rebates are a way to reduce the effective cost of an eligible prop firm purchase by receiving part of the applicable evaluation, challenge or programme fee back as cashback or another benefit.

Depending on the prop firm, programme and partnership arrangement, rebates may be calculated as:

  • a percentage of an eligible challenge or evaluation fee;
  • a fixed cashback amount per qualifying purchase;
  • a percentage of the partner commission received by FXRebate;
  • a direct discount or coupon applied at checkout;
  • an account credit, free retry, reset or other programme-specific benefit; or
  • a CPA-based cashback amount after specified qualification requirements are met.

For example, if an eligible evaluation costs $500 and the applicable cashback is 10%, the estimated cashback would be $50. The effective purchase cost after cashback would therefore be $450, provided that all programme conditions are satisfied.

A prop firm rebate does not change the evaluation rules, profit target, drawdown limits, payout conditions or the likelihood of passing a challenge. It only reduces an eligible cost or provides another clearly stated benefit.

How Do Prop Firm Rebates Work with FXRebate?

FXRebate partners with prop firms through affiliate, referral and commercial partnership arrangements.

When you access a prop firm through an FXRebate referral link or use an eligible FXRebate referral method, the prop firm may attribute your purchase or account to FXRebate.

The prop firm may then pay FXRebate a commission based on eligible activity, such as:

  • a confirmed challenge or evaluation purchase;
  • an eligible instant-funding or funded-programme purchase;
  • completion of specified verification or account requirements;
  • activation of an eligible account;
  • achievement of a defined programme milestone; or
  • another prop-firm-specific commercial model.

Depending on the partnership arrangement, FXRebate returns part of the eligible revenue to the client as cashback, a direct discount, an account credit or another stated rebate benefit.

How Are Prop Firm Rebates Paid?

The method used to deliver prop firm cashback depends on the individual firm and partnership arrangement. Not every payment option is available with every prop firm.

Prop Firm Rebate Payment Options

Purchase-Based Cashback / Percentage RebateCashback is calculated as a percentage of an eligible challenge, evaluation or programme purchase. Once the prop firm confirms the transaction and partner commission, the corresponding cashback is credited according to the FXRebate payment schedule.
Fixed Cashback per Eligible PurchaseA predefined cashback amount is offered for a qualifying programme, account size or purchase type. The amount does not necessarily change in direct proportion to the purchase price.
Direct Discount or Coupon CodeThe benefit is applied immediately at checkout through an eligible referral link or coupon code. Instead of receiving a later cash payment, the client pays a lower purchase price.

04

When Will I Receive My Prop Firm Cashback?

Payment frequency depends on the prop firm and the applicable partnership arrangement.

A direct discount is normally applied at checkout. Cash rebates may be credited after the prop firm confirms the purchase, completes its validation process and reports the partner commission to FXRebate. Some CPA or milestone-based rewards may take longer because additional conditions must first be satisfied.

The individual prop firm page on FXRebate should indicate:

  • the rebate or discount method;
  • the qualifying programme and account sizes;
  • the expected validation period;
  • the payment frequency and supported payment method;
  • any minimum payment threshold; and
  • any relevant qualification or refund conditions.

Delays may occasionally occur where:

  • the prop firm has not yet issued its partner report;
  • the purchase is still being reviewed or remains within a refund period;
  • the firm subsequently adjusts or reverses partner commission;
  • the purchase was not correctly attributed to FXRebate;
  • the client has not completed the required verification or programme milestone;
  • a refund, cancellation, dispute or chargeback has been requested; or
  • additional eligibility checks are required.

FXRebate only pays cashback on purchases or qualifying events that the relevant prop firm confirms as eligible under the applicable partnership.

How Much Prop Firm Cashback Can I Earn?

Your potential cashback depends on the rebate structure offered for the selected prop firm, programme and purchase.

Prop firm rebates may be expressed as:

  • a percentage of an eligible purchase price;
  • a fixed cashback amount per challenge or programme;
  • a percentage of eligible partner commission;
  • a direct discount or coupon;
  • a non-cash account or programme benefit; or
  • a fixed CPA- or milestone-based reward.

Example: Percentage of Purchase Price

Suppose an eligible evaluation costs $500 and the applicable FXRebate cashback is 10%.

The estimated cashback would be:

$500 × 10% = $50 cashback

Example: Fixed Cashback

Suppose FXRebate offers a fixed $35 cashback for an eligible challenge. Once the purchase and all required conditions are confirmed, the estimated cashback would be $35 regardless of whether the published purchase price was $300 or $500, unless the offer states otherwise.

07YOUR QUESTIONSWill My Challenge or Programme Fee Increase Because I Use FXRebate?+

FXRebate does not increase an eligible challenge, evaluation or programme fee in order to create a cashback payment.

Under standard FXRebate arrangements, the cashback comes from the commercial commission paid to FXRebate by the prop firm, or from a direct discount made available under the partnership.

Some offers, coupon codes or promotional prices may not be combinable. Using a different third-party coupon or visiting another affiliate link before completing a purchase may also affect attribution and cashback eligibility.

Users should therefore compare the complete cost of a prop firm programme, including challenge fees, activation fees, reset or retry fees, platform or data charges and the applicable cashback, rather than looking only at the advertised account size or headline discount.

08YOUR QUESTIONSAre Prop Firm Rebates Legitimate?+

Prop firm rebates and discounts are commonly offered through affiliate and referral partnerships.

The prop firm compensates a partner for introducing eligible clients or purchases, and the rebate provider may return part of that compensation to the client.

However, prop firm programmes and rebate offers are not identical. Before participating, users should check:

  • how the cashback or discount is calculated;
  • which programmes, account sizes and purchase types are eligible;
  • whether the offer is available only to new clients;
  • whether a coupon code can be combined with cashback;
  • whether resets, retries, activations, add-ons or data fees are included;
  • when the rebate becomes payable;

  • whether refunds, cancellations or chargebacks invalidate the rebate;
  • whether restricted countries or payment methods are excluded; and
  • whether milestone, verification or anti-abuse conditions apply.

FXRebate aims to display the applicable cashback method, payment schedule and eligibility conditions for each prop firm as clearly as possible.

09YOUR QUESTIONSDoes the Rebate Apply to Every Programme and Account Size?+

Not necessarily.

Eligibility may differ between:

  • one-step and two-step evaluations;
  • instant-funding programmes;
  • Forex, CFD, futures or crypto prop programmes;
  • different account or notional account sizes;
  • new purchases and repeat purchases;
  • standard and promotional programmes;
  • resets, retries and add-ons; and
  • specific client jurisdictions or payment methods.

Always check the relevant FXRebate prop firm page for the programmes, account sizes and purchase types included in the cashback offer.

10YOUR QUESTIONSAre Resets, Activations, Data and Platform Fees Rebatable?+

Usually, prop firm cashback applies only to the purchase or qualifying event specifically identified in the published offer.

The following charges are generally separate unless the offer explicitly states otherwise:

  • reset or retry fees;
  • activation or funded-account fees;
  • monthly subscription fees;
  • market-data or exchange-data fees;
  • platform licence fees;
  • add-on or upgrade charges;
Show 3 more itemsShow fewer items+
  • withdrawal or payout-processing charges;
  • currency conversion charges; and
  • third-party payment-processing fees.

These costs should not be assumed to qualify for cashback unless they are specifically included in the relevant FXRebate offer.

11YOUR QUESTIONSCan I Combine FXRebate Cashback with a Prop Firm Coupon or Promotion?+

It depends on the prop firm and the specific offer.

Some prop firms allow an FXRebate referral and a promotional coupon to be used together. Others permit only one discount, affiliate code or referral source per purchase. Using an unrelated coupon or visiting another affiliate link may overwrite FXRebate attribution and make the purchase ineligible for cashback.

Before completing the purchase, check the relevant FXRebate offer and follow the displayed referral instructions exactly.

12YOUR QUESTIONSWhat Happens If I Cancel, Refund or Charge Back the Purchase?+

If a qualifying purchase is cancelled, refunded, disputed or charged back, the prop firm may reverse the associated partner commission.

In that case, any related cashback may be withheld, cancelled or deducted from a future FXRebate balance. Non-cash benefits, account credits or promotional rewards may also be withdrawn according to the applicable terms.

Important Information

Prop firm cashback availability, calculation methods and eligibility vary by prop firm, legal entity, client jurisdiction, programme, account size, purchase type, payment method and partnership arrangement.

Certain clients, transactions, programmes, countries, payment methods or promotional purchases may not qualify.

Prop firms may also amend:

  • programme prices;
  • evaluation and trading rules;
  • discount and coupon conditions;
  • refund policies;
  • payout and profit-split conditions;
  • supported jurisdictions;
  • partner commission structures; or
  • cashback eligibility requirements.

Cashback does not guarantee that a client will pass an evaluation, receive a funded account, avoid a rule breach or receive a payout. Some prop firm programmes use simulated trading accounts, and the term “funded account” does not always mean that the client is trading live company capital.

Loyalty rewards, affiliate commissions, promotions, contests and CPA or milestone-based cashback may be subject to separate programme-specific terms.

Always refer to the cashback information displayed on the relevant FXRebate prop firm page and the applicable prop firm and FXRebate programme terms before purchasing a challenge or programme.
