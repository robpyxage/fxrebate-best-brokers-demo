01

What Are Forex Rebates?

Forex rebates are a way to reduce your effective trading costs by receiving part of the commission generated from your eligible trading activity back as cashback.

Depending on the broker, account type and trading instrument, rebates may be calculated as a fixed amount per lot, a number of pips, a percentage of the trading commission, or another broker-specific formula.

For example, if the trading cost associated with a position is equivalent to 3 pips and you receive a rebate equivalent to 1 pip, your effective cost after the rebate is reduced to the equivalent of 2 pips.

The rebate does not change whether an individual trade wins or loses. Instead, it reduces eligible trading costs, which can improve your overall net trading result over time, all other factors being equal.

How Do Forex Rebates Work with FXRebate?

FXRebate partners with Forex and CFD brokers through Introducing Broker, affiliate and referral agreements.

When you open a new account through FXRebate, or link an eligible existing account where the broker permits it, the broker attributes your account to FXRebate.

The broker may then pay FXRebate a commission based on your eligible trading activity. Depending on the commercial arrangement, FXRebate returns part of that revenue to you in the form of cashback or another rebate benefit.

How Are Forex Rebates Paid?

The method used to deliver your rebate depends on the individual broker and partnership arrangement. Not every payment option is available with every broker.

Rebate Payment Options

Rebate MethodHow It Works
Cash Rebate / Volume-Based CashbackYour rebate is calculated from eligible trading activity, such as the number of lots or trading volume completed during the relevant period. Once the broker confirms the eligible activity and partner commission, the corresponding cashback is credited according to the FXRebate payment schedule.
Spread or Commission ReductionSome brokers provide the benefit directly through reduced spreads or lower trading commissions. Instead of receiving a separate cash payment, the trader benefits from lower transaction costs directly on the trading account.
Rebate Paid Directly to the Trading AccountWith some brokers, the rebate is credited directly by the broker to the client's trading account. The credit frequency can vary from broker to broker and may be daily, weekly, monthly or based on another predefined schedule.
CPA-Based CashbackSome broker partnerships use a CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) model instead of a rebate calculated from ongoing trading volume. Under a CPA programme, the broker pays a fixed partner commission after a referred client satisfies specified qualification requirements. Where FXRebate offers CPA-based cashback to the client, an eligible portion or fixed amount is paid after the broker confirms that all required conditions have been met.

04

When Will I Receive My Rebate?

Payment frequency depends on the broker and rebate arrangement.

Some rebates can be credited shortly after eligible trades are confirmed, while others are calculated and paid on a monthly basis after FXRebate receives and reconciles the broker's commission report.

The individual broker page on FXRebate should indicate the available rebate method and, where applicable, the expected payment schedule.

Delays may occasionally occur where:

  • the broker has not yet issued its commission statement;
  • trading activity is still being verified;
  • the broker subsequently adjusts or reverses commission;
  • the account has not been correctly attributed to FXRebate; or
  • additional eligibility checks are required.

FXRebate only pays rebates on trading activity or CPA events confirmed as eligible under the applicable broker partnership.

05

How Much Cashback Can I Earn?

Your potential cashback depends on the rebate structure offered for your broker, account and trading instruments.

Rebates may be expressed as:

  • a fixed amount per lot;
  • an amount per USD 1 million traded;
  • pips per lot;
  • a percentage of commissions paid;
  • a percentage of eligible partner commission;
  • another volume-based formula; or
  • a fixed CPA-based cashback amount.

For example, if an eligible account provides a rebate of $2.50 per lot and you trade 100 eligible lots, the estimated rebate would be:

100 lots × $2.50 = $250 cashback

Active traders can use the FXRebate Rebate Calculator to estimate potential cashback based on their trading activity.

07YOUR QUESTIONSWill My Spread or Commission Increase Because I Use FXRebate?+

FXRebate does not increase your trading spread or commission in order to create a cashback payment.

For standard FXRebate rebate arrangements, the cashback comes from the partner commission paid to FXRebate by the broker rather than from an additional charge added to your trading account.

Where a particular broker, account type or partnership operates under different pricing conditions, these conditions should be clearly disclosed as part of the relevant broker offer.

This is why it is important to compare the complete trading conditions of an account — including spreads, commissions, swaps and rebates — rather than looking only at the headline cashback rate.

08YOUR QUESTIONSAre Forex Rebates Legitimate?+

Forex rebates are a common feature of Introducing Broker and affiliate arrangements within the retail trading industry.

The broker compensates a referring partner for introducing or supporting eligible clients, and the rebate provider can return part of that compensation to the trader.

However, rebate programmes are not identical. Traders should always check:

  • how the rebate is calculated;
  • which account types are eligible;
  • whether specific instruments are excluded;
  • whether there are minimum trading requirements;
  • how frequently rebates are calculated and paid;
  • whether any payment fees apply; and
  • whether the broker or rebate provider changes the normal trading conditions.

FXRebate aims to display the applicable rebate terms for each broker as clearly as possible so that clients can understand how their cashback is calculated.

09YOUR QUESTIONSCan Existing Broker Accounts Be Linked to FXRebate?+

Sometimes, but not with every broker.

Some brokers permit an eligible existing trading account to be transferred or linked to the FXRebate partner group. Other brokers require the client to open a new account through the FXRebate referral link.

Where an existing account is already linked to another Introducing Broker or affiliate, the broker may require approval from the current partner or may refuse the transfer under its internal rules.

Adding an account number to an FXRebate profile does not by itself confirm attribution. Rebates can only be activated after the broker or FXRebate verifies that the account is correctly linked and eligible under the applicable partnership.

10YOUR QUESTIONSDoes the Rebate Apply to All Instruments and Account Types?+

Not necessarily.

Eligibility and rebate rates may differ between:

  • standard, raw-spread, ECN, professional or other account types;
  • Forex currency pairs;
  • metals, energies, indices and commodities;
  • share CFDs;
  • cryptocurrency CFDs;
  • futures or other derivative products;
  • different broker legal entities; and
  • specific client jurisdictions or classifications.

A broker may pay different partner commissions for different products, or may exclude certain instruments entirely. Always check the relevant FXRebate broker page for the eligible accounts, instruments and applicable calculation method.

11YOUR QUESTIONSAre Deposits, Withdrawals, Swaps and Other Charges Rebatable?+

Usually, Forex cashback relates to eligible trading activity or a qualifying CPA event.

The following costs are generally separate unless an offer explicitly states otherwise:

  • deposit or withdrawal charges;
  • payment-processing fees;
  • currency conversion charges;
  • swap or overnight financing charges;
  • inactivity or account-maintenance fees;
  • market-data, VPS or platform charges;
  • slippage or execution differences; and
  • taxes or legally required withholding.

These costs should not be assumed to qualify for rebates unless they are specifically included in the published FXRebate broker offer.

Important Information

Rebate availability, calculation methods and eligibility vary by broker, legal entity, account type, trading instrument, client classification and jurisdiction.

Certain trades, account types, trading strategies or jurisdictions may not qualify for rebates. Brokers may also amend their partnership terms, commission structures or eligibility conditions.

Loyalty rewards, affiliate commissions, promotions, contests and CPA-based cashback may be subject to separate eligibility criteria and programme-specific terms.

Rebate amounts are based on activity and commission that the broker confirms as eligible. Cancelled, corrected, abusive, non-compliant or subsequently reversed trading activity may result in the related rebate being adjusted, withheld or reversed.

A displayed rebate rate does not guarantee a particular trading outcome and should not be considered a recommendation to trade more frequently or to choose a broker solely because of its cashback offer.

Always refer to the rebate information displayed on the relevant FXRebate broker page and the applicable broker or FXRebate programme terms before opening or linking an account.
