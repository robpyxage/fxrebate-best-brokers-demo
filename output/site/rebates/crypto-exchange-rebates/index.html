What Are Crypto Exchange Rebates?

Crypto exchange rebates are a way to reduce your effective trading costs by receiving part of the eligible trading fees generated through your activity back as cashback.

Depending on the exchange, account type, market and partnership arrangement, rebates may be calculated as:

  • a percentage of eligible maker or taker fees;
  • a fixed amount based on trading volume;
  • a percentage of the partner commission received by FXRebate;
  • a direct trading-fee discount;
  • a fixed promotional reward; or
  • a CPA-based cashback amount after specific qualification requirements are met.

For example, if an exchange charges a trading fee of 0.10% and the applicable cashback is equivalent to 20% of that eligible fee, the effective cost after cashback would be reduced to approximately 0.08%, subject to the exchange’s calculation and eligibility rules.

A rebate does not change whether an individual trade is profitable or unprofitable. Instead, it reduces eligible transaction costs, which can improve the trader’s overall net result over time, all other factors being equal.

How Do Crypto Exchange Rebates Work with FXRebate?

FXRebate partners with crypto exchanges through affiliate, referral and commercial partnership arrangements.

When you create a new exchange account through an FXRebate referral link—or link an eligible existing account where the exchange specifically permits it—the exchange attributes your account to FXRebate.

The exchange may then pay FXRebate a commission based on eligible activity, such as:

  • spot trading volume;
  • margin trading volume;
  • futures or perpetual-contract trading volume;
  • qualifying trading fees;
  • completion of specified account requirements; or
  • another exchange-specific commercial model.

Depending on the partnership arrangement, FXRebate returns part of the eligible revenue to the client as cashback, a fee reduction or another rebate benefit.

How Are Crypto Exchange Rebates Paid?

The method used to deliver crypto rebates depends on the individual exchange and partnership arrangement. Not every payment option is available with every exchange.

Crypto Rebate Payment Options

Rebate MethodHow It Works
Trading-Fee Cashback / Volume-Based RebateCashback is calculated from eligible trading activity, such as spot or derivatives volume, or from eligible maker and taker fees paid during the relevant period. Once the exchange confirms the activity and partner commission, the corresponding cashback is credited according to the FXRebate payment schedule.
Direct Trading-Fee ReductionSome exchanges apply the benefit directly by reducing the eligible trading fee. Instead of receiving a separate payment, the client pays a lower effective maker or taker fee.

Under a CPA programme, the exchange pays a fixed partner commission after a referred client satisfies defined qualification requirements. Where FXRebate offers CPA-based cashback, an eligible portion or fixed amount is paid after the exchange confirms that all required conditions have been met.

04

When Will I Receive My Crypto Rebate?

Payment frequency depends on the exchange and the applicable partnership arrangement.

Some crypto rebates may be calculated shortly after eligible trades are confirmed. Others may be credited daily, weekly or monthly after FXRebate receives and reconciles the exchange’s partner report.

The individual crypto exchange page on FXRebate should indicate:

  • the rebate method;
  • the expected calculation period;
  • the payment frequency;
  • the supported payment currency;
  • any minimum payment threshold; and
  • any relevant eligibility conditions.

Delays may occasionally occur where:

  • the exchange has not yet issued its commission or activity report;
  • trading activity is still being reviewed;
  • the exchange subsequently adjusts or reverses partner commission;
  • the account was not correctly attributed to FXRebate;
  • the user has not completed all required verification steps;
  • the exchange is investigating abusive or prohibited activity; or
  • additional eligibility checks are required.

FXRebate only pays cashback on activity or CPA events that the relevant exchange confirms as eligible under the applicable partnership.

How Much Crypto Cashback Can I Earn?

Your potential cashback depends on the rebate structure offered for the selected exchange, product and trading activity.

Crypto rebates may be expressed as:

  • a percentage of eligible trading fees;
  • a fixed amount per USD, EUR, BTC or USDT of trading volume;
  • a percentage of eligible partner commission;
  • a direct fee discount;
  • a fixed promotional cashback amount; or
  • a fixed CPA-based reward.

Example: Percentage of Trading Fees

Suppose you generate $500 in eligible trading fees and the applicable FXRebate cashback equals 20% of those fees.

The estimated cashback would be:

$500 × 20% = $100 cashback

Example: Volume-Based Rebate

Suppose the applicable rebate is $4 per USD 1 million traded and you complete USD 25 million in eligible trading volume.

The estimated cashback would be:

25 × $4 = $100 cashback

Example: Monthly Activity

If your eligible activity generates approximately $100 cashback per month, your estimated annual cashback would be:

$100 × 12 = $1,200 per year

Users can use the FXRebate Crypto Rebate Calculator or Rebate Savings Calculator to estimate potential cashback based on their trading activity.

07YOUR QUESTIONSWill My Trading Fees Increase Because I Use FXRebate?+

FXRebate does not increase your exchange trading fees in order to create a cashback payment.

Under standard FXRebate arrangements, the rebate comes from the commercial commission paid to FXRebate by the exchange, rather than from an additional fee added to the client’s account.

Where an exchange offers a direct fee discount, the benefit may be applied automatically to eligible trading fees.

However, the final cost of trading may still depend on several factors, including:

  • maker and taker fee levels;
  • spot or derivatives fee schedules;
  • VIP or volume tiers;
  • exchange-token discounts;
  • funding payments on perpetual contracts;
  • borrowing interest for margin positions;

  • deposit or withdrawal fees;
  • blockchain network fees;
  • liquidation fees;
  • currency conversion costs; and
  • the applicable FXRebate cashback.

For this reason, traders should compare the complete cost of using an exchange rather than looking only at the advertised cashback percentage.

08YOUR QUESTIONSAre Crypto Exchange Rebates Legitimate?+

Crypto exchange rebates are commonly offered through affiliate and referral partnerships.

The exchange compensates a partner for introducing or supporting eligible clients, and the rebate provider may return part of that compensation to the client.

However, not all rebate programmes operate in the same way. Before participating, users should check:

  • how the rebate is calculated;
  • whether it applies to maker fees, taker fees or both;
  • which products and markets are eligible;
  • whether spot, margin and derivatives activity are treated differently;
  • whether VIP or discounted fee tiers affect the rebate;
  • whether trading with an exchange token changes eligibility;

  • how frequently rebates are calculated and credited;
  • whether minimum withdrawal or payment thresholds apply;
  • whether restricted countries or client categories are excluded; and
  • whether promotional or abusive trading activity is ineligible.

FXRebate aims to display the applicable cashback method, payment schedule and eligibility conditions for each crypto exchange as clearly as possible.

Important Information

Crypto rebate availability, calculation methods and eligibility vary by exchange, legal entity, client jurisdiction, account status, product, market, trading-fee tier and partnership arrangement.

Certain users, transactions, trading strategies, countries, products or account types may not qualify.

Exchanges may also amend:

  • fee schedules;
  • VIP-tier conditions;
  • cashback calculations;
  • referral rules;
  • supported jurisdictions;
  • partner commission structures;
  • listed products; or
  • eligibility conditions.

Loyalty rewards, affiliate commissions, promotions, contests and CPA-based cashback may be subject to separate programme-specific terms.

The value of a cashback payment made in a cryptocurrency or digital asset may fluctuate after it is credited.

Always refer to the cashback information displayed on the relevant FXRebate crypto exchange page and the applicable exchange and FXRebate programme terms before opening an account, depositing funds or trading.
