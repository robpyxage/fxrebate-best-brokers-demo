## Limited editorial verification
The following examples were checked against official material on 16 November 2026. This is a spot check, not a full legal, numerical or provider-terms audit; Sergiu's wording and examples remain in the pages. Full source notes remain in the respective documents.
- Forex: BIS confirms approximately USD 9.5 trillion daily global FX turnover for April 2025 in its December 2025 analysis: https://www.bis.org/publications/qr-202512/global-fx-markets-when-hedging-takes-centre-stage. BIS's earlier September 2025 preliminary release rounded to USD 9.6 trillion: https://www.bis.org/media-releases/20250930-global-fx-trading-hits-96-trillion-day-april-2025-and-otc-interest-rate-derivatives-surge-79. The supplied 9.5 trillion final figure is preserved.
- Forex OTC/retail risks: https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8566-22.
- Crypto: ESMA's consumer factsheet describes MiCA coverage, authorization checks and remaining limitations: https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-10/Joint_ESAs_Factsheet_on_crypto-assets.pdf.
- Prop: FTMO states its provided accounts use fictitious capital and rewards follow the applicable terms: https://ftmo.com/en/faq/what-capital-will-i-trade-on-an-ftmo-account/. This verifies the selected FTMO example, not all providers.
